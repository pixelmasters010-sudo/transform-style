# Transform Style

Build a premium, dark-themed website for a British luxury fashion brand that designs transformative garments — clothing that converts from one silhouette to another (e.g. a shirt that transforms into a dress). This is a high-end ecommerce concept site: sophisticated "quiet luxury" with warmth, naturalistic imagery, and a strong commercial focus on the products.

CRITICAL — HERO VIDEO MUST BE TASTEFUL, NOT SUGGESTIVE

- The transformation must read as a clever design/engineering feature of the garment (like origami or modular fashion), not as undressing or a striptease-style reveal

- The model should remain fully and modestly dressed throughout the entire clip — the "before" state is one fully-formed garment (e.g. a buttoned shirt), the "after" state is another fully-formed garment (e.g. a dress) — no bare skin exposed during the transition beyond what's shown in either end state

- Show the transformation via quick fabric folding/wrapping/clasping motions (hands adjusting fastenings, fabric panels folding into place) rather than anything resembling removing clothing

- Tone: architectural and clever, like watching a piece of furniture unfold — not a fashion-show runway moment and not intimate/bedroom in feel

- Lighting and framing should be bright, clean, and matter-of-fact — avoid moody/intimate lighting, close-up body shots, or slow-motion on the body itself; keep focus on the fabric and mechanism of transformation

- If unsure, err toward showing the transformation as a quick stop-motion-style sequence of the garment on a mannequin/flat surface rather than on a live model, then cut to the model wearing the finished look

AESTHETIC DIRECTION

- Dark theme: deep charcoal/near-black backgrounds (#0A0A0A – #121212), not pure black

- Accent: one warm muted tone (champagne gold, warm bone, terracotta-taupe), used sparingly

- Elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif body, comfortable line-lengths, conversational copy

- Naturalistic photography direction throughout: avoid glossy/synthetic AI-render look — natural lighting, real texture, candid rather than hyper-posed

- Subtle warmth: soft film-grain, gently rounded card corners, restrained use

HERO SECTION

- Short autoplaying looped background video (muted, no controls) per the tasteful transformation direction above

- Minimal overlay text, single CTA leading straight to Shop

- Keep brief — a doorway to shopping, not the main event

PAGE PRIORITY (products first)

1. Home — hero video → prominent featured products grid → "How It Transforms" showcase module (fabric/mechanism-focused, same tasteful direction as hero) → one-line story teaser linking to Our Story → email signup

2. Shop / Collection — full product grid, filterable (Shirts, Dresses, Convertible), large clear product imagery, hover-reveal second angle, visible pricing

3. Individual product page — large gallery, zoom, multiple angles, on-body shots (fully dressed, both end-states), transformation clip (same tasteful direction), size/fit guide, care instructions, prominent "Add to Bag"

4. How It Transforms — dedicated explainer page, step-by-step diagram-style breakdown of the folding/fastening mechanism, supplemented by video

5. Our Story — concise founder note, craftsmanship, sustainability angle — kept secondary to Shop

6. Journal / Lookbook — editorial styling stories, seasonal lookbooks

7. Contact / Stockists — simple form, social links

8. Footer — Instagram strip, newsletter signup, legal placeholders

GENERAL POLISH

- Consistent spacing/grid rhythm across all sections — no cramped or misaligned blocks

- Consistent corner radius, shadow, and hover-state treatment across all cards and buttons

- Ensure all CTAs use the same button style and accent colour throughout

- Double-check contrast — text must stay clearly legible on the dark background everywhere, not just in the hero

- Smooth, consistent transition/animation timing site-wide (no jarring speed differences between sections)

UX REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive, mobile-first

- Sticky navbar: logo, Shop, How It Transforms, Our Story, Journal, Contact

- Cart drawer (slide-in)

- Product grid never more than one click from the homepage hero

TONE FOR PLACEHOLDER COPY

Confident, warm, editorial for Story/Journal; practical and sales-focused for product copy (materials, fit, transformation instructions).

Generate 6-8 sample products (name, price in GBP, one-line description, materials line) themed around convertible/transformable garments, with naturalistic and tasteful imagery direction as above, and 2-3 sample Journal/Lookbook entries.
